1The bench

You find the enthalpy change for Mg(s) + ½O2(g) → MgO(s) without burning any magnesium: Part I measures your calorimeter, Parts II and III measure two reactions in it, and Hess’s law does the rest. Click a thing on the bench to pick it up and click where it goes; click it again to put it down. Every step also has a button in the list beside the bench.
